Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3) bounty
"Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line" — one sentence, up to 200
characters, on a small moment of wonder in everyday life.

## The entry

> The gecko on the bathroom wall froze mid-step as I brushed my teeth, and for a second the house felt less like mine and more like a place I was only visiting.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) writes that single
sentence to `/output/entry.txt`, after checking it is one sentence of
1–200 characters.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
